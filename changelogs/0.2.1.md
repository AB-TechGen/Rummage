# 0.2.1

- Rebalanced target selection into four tiers gated by both round count and active-round time. Unused goals are preferred, recent repeats are avoided, and later tiers carry more selection weight.
- Moved expensive resource blocks and the rarest targets later; rapid early completions cannot unlock them immediately. See docs/TARGET_SELECTION.md for exact pools, gates and weights.
- Fixed spectator/survival flicker by avoiding repeated permission/game-mode writes and preventing vanilla permission refreshes from overriding match-phase modes.
- Avoided redundant lookout teleports while preserving the fixed lookout position.
- Commands, LAN, game-mode and rule-edit controls are visibly disabled during matches.
- Difficulty remains adjustable between Easy, Normal and Hard; Peaceful becomes Easy before it is applied. New worlds default to Easy, and older match worlds are unlocked.
- Everyone in a lobby must use 0.2.1. The intermittent native launch crash remains separately deferred.

Validation: Gradle build passed with 613 assertions, including progression gates, deterministic weighted draws, repeat protection and active-time accounting. All 19 mixin target/shadow/argument-modifier checks passed. In-game UI and phase-transition verification remains outstanding.
